Privacy statement

Practice project. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who we are

Tostino is an invented coffee roastery, made as a practice project by Antonio Frungillo. No real business, orders or payments exist.

What this website stores

Nothing is sent to a server. Your browser keeps your chosen language and your cart in localStorage, and the confirmation of your practice order in sessionStorage until you close the tab. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Checkout details

The checkout is a simulation. What you type (name, email, address) stays in your browser, is shown on the confirmation page and is never sent anywhere.

Third parties

There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Fonts are hosted on this site. The map and GitHub links open OpenStreetMap or GitHub only if you click them, and their privacy rules then apply.

Contact

The contact form opens your own email app. We only receive a message if you send it. Questions: ciao@tostino.example.

If this becomes a real shop

A real payment provider, order storage and an email service would process personal data. This statement and the cookie policy would be updated first.
